# Changelog

## 1.4.1 — 2026-11-25
- Low Power Mode: the password is asked only once. If the first password prompt was cancelled, the app offers the no-password option again on the next tap instead of asking for a password every time.
- Allowing the no-password option now installs the rule and switches the mode in a single password prompt.

## 1.4.0 — 2026-11-24
- Interface in three languages: Українська, Русский, English (follows the system or choose in Settings).
- General Staff report translated into all three languages.

## 1.3.0
- On-screen volume / brightness indicator.
- Battery: white in normal mode, yellow in Low Power Mode; tap toggles Low Power Mode.
- Optional "Low Power Mode without password" (narrow sudoers rule, opt-in).
- Tap the time to open Calendar.
- **Bar items**: reorder or hide any item, including the embroidery.

## 1.1.0
- Tap the weather for a 7-day forecast on the Touch Bar; **Details** opens a full weather window (24 hours, 7 days).

## 1.0.1
- Layout adapts to Macs with a physical Esc key (16″ 2019, 13″ 2020, M1, M2).

## 1.0.0
- First public release: living embroidery, brightness, volume, esc, screenshot, weather for any city, battery, time, input language, General Staff report, live wallpaper, welcome window, launch at login.
